Frequently asked questions

What is AI privacy awareness training?expand_more

AI privacy awareness training teaches your team how to use generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini) without leaking client data, breaching confidentiality, or creating exposure under Canadian privacy laws like PIPEDA. Part technical, part policy, part habit-change.

Who needs AI privacy awareness training?expand_more

Any team where employees handle sensitive information — client records, patient notes, financial data, tenant data, proprietary documents — and have access to public AI tools. That covers most Canadian small businesses, clinics, property managers, and non-profits today.

How long does the training take?expand_more

A focused workshop typically runs 2–4 hours. Larger organizations or those needing follow-up policy work usually take a half-day session followed by 1–2 reinforcement sessions over a few weeks.

Does the training cover PIPEDA?expand_more

Yes — the training contextualizes AI use within PIPEDA's requirements around personal information handling, consent, and breach exposure. It is not a legal compliance audit, but it gives your team the working knowledge they need to avoid creating PIPEDA-relevant incidents.

How is this different from generic AI training?expand_more

Most AI training teaches people how to get more out of AI. This training teaches them how to get value from AI without exposing the things your business cannot afford to leak. The framing is privacy-first, with productivity wins built around safe defaults.
