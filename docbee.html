Local AI · Powered by Gemma 4

Generate and format documents without your data ever leaving your machine.

DocBee is a local AI tool that drafts, rewrites, and formats your documents directly on your hardware. No cloud uploads. No third-party servers. Just Gemma 4, running where your work lives.
